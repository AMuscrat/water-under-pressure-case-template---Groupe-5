About AquaCrop Decision Lab
Make every cubic metre count.

AquaCrop Decision Lab is a planning workspace for agricultural cooperatives in Southern Europe. It brings water availability, climate context and crop scenarios together so farmers and cooperative teams can compare trade-offs before committing to a season.

Use it to test assumptions, understand water pressure and identify a practical crop-water direction. The prototype is designed for decision support and learning, not as a certified agronomic forecast.
