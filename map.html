Data note: Country outlines and NUTS-2 region polygons are sourced from Eurostat GISCO GeoJSON, projected together, and baked into data/europe-geo-data.js at development time by scripts/build-map-data.mjs. Drought pressure and seven-day rainfall are derived from Open-Meteo forecasts and stored as a build-time snapshot in data/region-climate-live.js, with an asOf timestamp. WEI+ and irrigated utilised agricultural area use Eurostat SDG 06_60 and tai03 national figures applied to every mapped region in the relevant country; this is national-scope evidence, not a region-specific claim. Albania remains unavailable where Eurostat returns no observation. Malta and Gozo (MT-M / MT-G) share the same NUTS-2 boundary (MT00), so they render as the same shape on this map.
