07 · SHOULD I TRUST THIS?

Real weather, transparent prototype logic.

Explainable by design

Open-Meteo provides live ET₀, precipitation, temperature, and soil-moisture observations. Global Water Watch provides live reservoir observations. Irrigation availability is a derived estimate combining reservoir position, soil moisture, rainfall, and ET₀; it is not an official allocation or permit. Crop demand and recommendation scoring are transparent prototype calculations, not calibrated agronomic or financial advice.

Show calculation notes

Mean ET₀ adjusts crop demand within a bounded range. Sliders recalculate supply, demand, buffer, crop impacts, and the single recommendation immediately.
