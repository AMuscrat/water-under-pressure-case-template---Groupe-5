06 · METHODOLOGY

What this prototype assumes

Explainable by design
01

Live weather layer

Open-Meteo geocoding resolves the selected Southern European location. A seven-day forecast provides ET₀, precipitation, and maximum temperature for the current scenario.

02

Mock water constraints

Reservoir level, soil moisture, and cooperative allocation remain clearly labelled mock regional indicators for the university prototype.

03

Scenario adjustment

Live mean ET₀ scales the crop water requirement within a bounded range. Heat / drought pressure can still be adjusted manually to test decisions.

04

Decision score

The recommendation balances water fit, reserve buffer, and a preference for resilient crop coverage. It is not a calibrated agronomic model.

Important: live weather is real-time public data, but the recommendation logic and regional allocation assumptions are a university prototype. Do not use it as agronomic or financial advice.
