05 · METHODOLOGY

What this prototype assumes

Explainable by design
01

Mock irrigation demand

Each crop gets a simplified seasonal water demand per hectare. These values are illustrative and are not a calibrated AquaCrop simulation.

02

Stress multiplier

Heat / drought pressure increases crop demand and reduces the available buffer to represent a more difficult summer.

03

Decision score

The recommendation balances water fit, a reserve buffer, and a preference for diversified, high-value crop coverage.

04

Next data layer

A future version can connect to public drought, rainfall, soil, crop, and water-allocation data and retain the same decision workflow.

Important: numbers shown in this demo are mock data for a university prototype. They should not be used as agronomic or financial advice.
